# Mock Test (Пробник) Implementation Plan

**Goal:** a Reading and Writing mock inside the trainer: two modules of 27 questions and 32 minutes, exam order, the second module harder or easier by the first, no help on the way, and at the end a score range out of 800 with a breakdown.

**Architecture:** `assets/mock.js` holds the pure parts (building a module, the route, the score, the result) and the screens, created like `prep.js` with a context from `app.js`. The view `S.ui.view = "mock"` has its own renderer: the trainer's gate, think-first steps and feedback are not in it. Answers are written to `S.progress` only when the mock ends; mistakes go to the review queue. Finished mocks are kept by id in `KEY.mocks` and synced as the account section `mocks`.

**Spec (agreed in chat 2026-11-11):**
- questions: not answered yet first; if a type runs short, the ones answered longest ago;
- a module: Craft and Structure 7 (Words in Context 4, Text Structure and Purpose 2, Cross-Text Connections 1), Information and Ideas 7 (Central Ideas and Details 2, Command of Evidence 3, Inferences 2), Standard English Conventions 7 (Boundaries 3, Form, Structure, and Sense 4), Expression of Ideas 6 (Transitions 3, Rhetorical Synthesis 3);
- order: the blocks in that order; inside a block by type, then easier first; grammar by difficulty alone;
- module 1 mixed, Medium where it can be; ≥ 2/3 right → module 2 harder (Hard first), else easier (Easy/Medium);
- as on the test: navigation inside a module, «отметить», striking out choices, a 32-minute countdown that can be hidden, a warning at 5 minutes, the module closes itself at 0:00;
- score: right / total → raw out of 66 → the official range of College Board's «Scoring Your SAT Practice Test #7» (paper version of the digital test). Checked against the user's Bluebook mock: 40 of 54 → 580–640, Bluebook said 630. An easier second module gets a note that on the real test it caps the score;
- result: per module, per type (right, rushed, longer than the norm), the wrong questions with a link.

## Task 1: pure functions — `tests/mock.test.js`
`buildModule(questions, progress, { level: "mixed"|"harder"|"easier", exclude: Set, rng })` → ordered ids; `routeOf(correct, total)`; `scoreRange(correct, total)` → `[low, high]`; `resultOf(modules, byId)` → `{ correct, total, perModule, skills }`.
Tests: blueprint counts and block order; Medium first in module 1, Hard in the harder module; unanswered before answered, oldest answered first; no repeats across modules; order inside a block; route at 18/27; 40/54 → 580–640, 54/54 → 800, 0 → 200; a small bank gives a shorter module without duplicates; unanswered count as wrong.
